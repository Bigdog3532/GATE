🚀 What Week 1 Is About
Week 1 is NOT about studying syllabus topics. It's about building the system that will carry you for 9 months. You set your alarm, you take your diagnostic test, you gather resources, you build your schedule habit. If you do Week 1 right, every week after is easier. Start small. Start today.

WORK DAY
0%
▾

Self assessment of every GATE CS subject. Be brutally honest. "I used it at Wipro" ≠ "I know it well enough for GATE."

🎧 Evening — Light + First Aptitude
Download/bookmark your aptitude app. Do your first 10 questions. This habit starts tonight.
— MORNING: SUBJECT SELF-RATING (Be brutally honest)
✓
Open your notebook. Write all 10 subjects. For each, rate: Strong (S) / Average (A) / Weak (W). Do this from memory — don't Google anything while rating.
20 min
✓

✓

10 min
✓

10 min
— EVENING TASKS (8:45–9:30 PM)

Download "Testbook" or "MathonGo" app for aptitude. Do your first 10 questions (verbal 5 + numerical 5). Note your score. This is your aptitude baseline.
20 min
✓
Watch one NPTEL intro lecture — search "NPTEL algorithms IIT Bombay" on YouTube. Just watch 20 min to gauge the quality and your comfort level with the content.
25 min

06
Saturday — THE DIAGNOSTIC TEST
3-hour real GATE paper. Your baseline score. The most important task of Week 1.
TEST DAY
0%
▾
8:00 AM
☕ Wake Up + Prep
Light breakfast. Water. Clear desk. GATE 2023 paper printed or on a tablet. Phone in another room. No reference material allowed.
8:30 AM
📝 GATE CS 2023 — Full 3-Hour Diagnostic Test
65 questions. 3 hours. Zero help. Zero breaks. This is exactly how the real exam will feel. Attempt every section. Write your answers on paper. Note which questions you skipped or guessed.
11:30 AM
🍱 Lunch + Rest — Do NOT check answers yet
Eat, relax for 1 hour. Let your brain decompress before analysis. Don't peek at the answer key yet.
12:30 PM
🔍 Deep Analysis — 2.5 Hours
Grade every answer. Calculate your approximate score. Map mistakes to subjects. This analysis is more important than the test itself.
5:00 PM
🎮 Free Evening — You've Earned It
Rest. No study. Mental recovery matters. Tomorrow is light — just planning.
— BEFORE TEST (8:00–8:30 AM)
✓
Clear your desk. Print or open GATE CS 2023 paper. Keep: blank paper for rough work, pen/pencil, water. Phone OFF and in another room.
Pre-test
✓
Set a 3-hour timer. Write start time on paper. This is a simulation — treat it exactly like the real exam day.
Pre-test
— DURING TEST (8:30–11:30 AM)
✓
Complete the 3-hour test. For each question: attempt, mark "confident/unsure/skipped." Don't leave any NAT blank — write your best estimate.
3 hours
— ANALYSIS (12:30–3:00 PM)
✓
Grade all answers. Calculate approximate GATE marks = (1-mark correct × 1) + (2-mark correct × 2) − (MCQ wrong × ⅓ or ⅔). Write your total.
30 min
✓
Subject-wise breakdown — how many marks did you get in: GA, Maths, DSA, OS, TOC, DBMS, CN, COA, C Prog, Compiler, DL? Write all in a table.
30 min
✓
For every wrong answer — read the correct solution on GATE Overflow. Don't try to re-study now, just understand the answer. Mark for future study.
60 min
✓
Write 3 conclusions: (1) My baseline score is ___ marks. (2) My 3 strongest subjects are ___. (3) My 3 weakest subjects that need most time are ___.
15 min
Expected score range: Most working professionals score 15–30 marks in a cold diagnostic — that is completely normal and expected. Don't be disheartened. This score will grow by 30–40 marks over 9 months of focused prep. The gap you see today is exactly what you're going to close.
